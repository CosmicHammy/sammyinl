Frequently Asked Questions

Our regular school hours are from 8:30 AM to 3:15 PM for all grades. Preschool has a half-day option ending at 12:30 PM. Extended care is available from 7:30 AM to 6:00 PM for an additional fee.

Tuition varies by grade level:

  • Preschool: $1,250 - $1500 annually
  • Elementary (1-5): $1800 annually
  • Middle School (6-8): $2000 annually

We offer sibling discounts and need-based financial aid. Please contact our admissions office for detailed fee structures and payment plans.

We maintain low student-teacher ratios to ensure personalized attention:

  • Preschool: 8:1
  • Kindergarten - 2nd Grade: 12:1
  • 3rd Grade - 5th Grade: 15:1
  • Middle School: 18:1
